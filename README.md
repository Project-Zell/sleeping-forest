## About the Game

[In to the Woods] is a survival action game inspired by the **Vampire Survivors** genre, developed during a game jam by a team of 10 people.

The game follows a child who has become lost in the woods. Surrounded by strange creatures born from their imagination, the child must fight to survive using an expanding arsenal of weapons and abilities.

As the journey progresses, players face different types of enemies, acquire upgrades, and develop their character to stand a better chance against the creatures lurking in the forest.

## Key Features

- **Survivor-Style Gameplay** – Fight against waves of enemies in an action-focused survival experience.
- **Multiple Enemy Types** – Encounter different imaginary monsters, each presenting its own threat.
- **Weapon Variety** – Unlock and use multiple weapons to fight off the creatures.
- **Upgrade System** – Improve weapons and abilities to become more effective in combat.
- **Character Progression** – Develop your character throughout the run and adapt to increasingly difficult encounters.
